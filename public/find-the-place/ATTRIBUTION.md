# Data attribution

French region, department, commune, population, and center-coordinate data is
derived from the French government's
[API Découpage administratif](https://geo.api.gouv.fr/decoupage-administratif),
retrieved on 2026-08-16.

The German, Italian, Russian, and Spanish place names, coordinates, and
administrative relationships are derived from the
[GeoNames geographical database](https://www.geonames.org/), downloaded on
2026-08-16. GeoNames data is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The Indian, Brazilian, Mexican, Australian, Japanese, and additional European
place names, coordinates, populations, and first-level administrative
relationships are derived from GeoNames country exports downloaded on
2026-08-21. Japanese place and prefecture labels use the Japanese-language
names from the GeoNames alternate-name export. Vatican City uses two named
GeoNames landmarks because the country contains only one populated-place
record. These data are also licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 License linked above.
