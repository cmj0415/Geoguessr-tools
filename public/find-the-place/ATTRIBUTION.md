# Data attribution

Find the Place names, coordinates, populations, and administrative
relationships are derived from the
[GeoNames geographical database](https://www.geonames.org/). The online quiz
catalog is refreshed weekly from GeoNames country, administrative-code, and
alternate-name exports. Japanese place and prefecture labels use
Japanese-language alternate names.
