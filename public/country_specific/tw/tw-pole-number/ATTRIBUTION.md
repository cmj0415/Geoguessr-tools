# Taiwan Pole Number Data Attribution

The land-qualified question pool in `cells.json` was generated from the
National Land Surveying and Mapping Center's **直轄市、縣市界線 (TWD97經緯度)**
county-boundary dataset:

- Source: https://maps.nlsc.gov.tw/pro/download.jsp
- Direct download: https://maps.nlsc.gov.tw/download/縣市界線(TWD97經緯度).zip
- Provider: National Land Surveying and Mapping Center, Ministry of the Interior, Taiwan
- License: Government Open Data License, version 1.0

Taiwan Power Company grid definitions and conversion parameters follow:

- https://wiki.osgeo.org/wiki/Taiwan_Power_Company_grid
- https://www.jidanni.org/geo/taipower/programs/taipowergrid
